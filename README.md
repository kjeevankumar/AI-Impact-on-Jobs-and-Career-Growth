AI Impact on Jobs and Career Growth Dashboard

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard that explores how Artificial Intelligence is influencing job opportunities, salary trends, and career growth across different roles and industries.

The idea behind this project is to go beyond simple data visualization and present meaningful insights that reflect real-world changes happening in the job market due to AI.

Project Overview

The dashboard is designed to analyze the relationship between AI adoption and key career factors such as salary, job demand, and automation risk. It helps in understanding which roles are growing, which are at risk, and how skills in AI are shaping future careers.

Key Objectives
To analyze job demand trends over time
To identify high-paying AI-related roles
To study salary growth based on experience levels
To understand automation risk across different job roles
To explore the impact of AI specialization on career opportunities
Dashboard Structure

The dashboard consists of three main sections:

1. Executive Overview

This section provides a summary of the overall job market, including total jobs, average salary, AI adoption score, and employee satisfaction. It also highlights job demand trends and industry distribution.

2. Salary and Career Growth Insights

This section focuses on compensation and career progression. It includes salary comparisons across roles, salary growth by experience level, and geographic distribution of salaries.

3. AI Impact and Automation Insights

This section provides deeper analysis of AI influence. It shows AI adoption trends, automation risks across roles, and the relationship between AI adoption, salary, and job demand. It also highlights how different AI specializations impact job roles.

Tools and Technologies Used
Power BI for dashboard development
DAX for data calculations and measures
Data modeling and transformation techniques
Key Insights
AI adoption has been increasing steadily over time
Roles with higher AI adoption tend to offer higher salaries
Some job roles show higher automation risk despite strong demand
Experience level plays a major role in salary growth
AI specialization significantly influences career opportunities
What Makes This Project Unique

This project combines multiple perspectives such as job trends, salary analysis, and AI impact into a single dashboard. It is designed not just for visualization, but for understanding real-world patterns and making data-driven decisions.

The dashboard also follows a structured and professional design approach, making it suitable for business-level analysis.

Future Improvements
Integration with real-time datasets
Adding predictive analytics for job trends
Enhancing interactivity with advanced filters
Deploying the dashboard as a web application
